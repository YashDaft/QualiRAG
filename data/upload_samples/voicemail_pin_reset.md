# KB-1086: Resetting Your Voicemail PIN

Applies to: Employees with a Brightwick desk phone number
Owner: Messaging | Last reviewed: 2026-10-28

## About voicemail
Every Brightwick desk phone number has a voicemail box. You can listen to messages from your desk phone, from Microsoft Teams, or by calling extension 6245 from any Brightwick phone. Voicemail messages are deleted automatically after 30 days.

## Your voicemail PIN
Your voicemail PIN is 6 digits long. It cannot be the same as your extension number, and it cannot use the same digit six times, such as 111111.

## If you forget your PIN
1. Sign in to MyAccess and open **Phone settings**.
2. Choose **Reset voicemail PIN**. A temporary PIN is shown on screen and is valid for 24 hours.
3. Call extension 6245, enter the temporary PIN, and choose a new one when prompted.

## If your voicemail is locked
Your voicemail box locks after 5 wrong PIN attempts in a row. It unlocks on its own after 30 minutes, or you can unlock it straight away by resetting the PIN in MyAccess.

If MyAccess doesn't show **Phone settings**, your number may not be set up for voicemail. Raise a P4 request in ServicePoint under "Phone and voicemail".
