# KB-1075: Meeting Room Screens and Video Calls at Headquarters

Applies to: Headquarters staff
Owner: Workplace Technology | Last reviewed: 2026-10-14

## Room types
Headquarters has two kinds of meeting room:
- **Huddle rooms** (2 to 6 people) have a wall screen and a single USB-C cable on the table. There is no room console. You join the call from your own laptop.
- **Conference rooms** (8 people or more) have a Teams Rooms console on the table, a ceiling camera and ceiling microphones. The console joins the meeting for the whole room.

Room names start with the floor number, for example 3.04 Ashdown is a conference room on Level 3.

## Booking a room
Book rooms from the Room Finder in Outlook, the same way you add a person to a meeting. A booking is released automatically if nobody checks in on the panel outside the room within 15 minutes of the start time. Recurring bookings are limited to 12 weeks, after which you need to book again.

## Joining a call in a conference room
1. Tap **Join** next to your meeting on the console screen. Only meetings that include the room as a guest appear there.
2. To share your screen, connect the USB-C cable to your laptop, or open the meeting on your laptop and choose **Share** with your laptop's audio and microphone turned off.
3. Leave the call from the console when you finish. The console resets the room for the next booking.

External visitors cannot use the console. They can present by connecting the USB-C cable to their own laptop, which shows their screen without giving them access to the room's camera or the Brightwick network.

## If something isn't working
| Problem | What to try |
|---|---|
| Screen shows "No signal" | Unplug the USB-C cable for 5 seconds and plug it back in. On a huddle room screen, check the input is set to USB-C. |
| Others can't hear the room | Check the microphone icon on the console isn't red (muted). Turn off your laptop's microphone so it doesn't echo. |
| Console is frozen | Tap **More**, then **Restart room**. Do not unplug the console, because it takes about 10 minutes to recover. |

If the room still doesn't work, call the IT Service Desk on extension 4357 and give the room name. A room fault that stops a meeting starting within the next hour is logged as a P3 and sent to the IT Bar, which aims to have someone at the room within 20 minutes during IT Bar hours.
