# Password Standard

Document ID: POL-SEC-PW-002 | Version: 2.1 | Effective date: 2023-01-15
Owner: IT Security | Classification: Internal
Status: SUPERSEDED on 2026-03-01 by the Access Control Policy v3.0 (POL-SEC-002). Retained for reference only. Do not follow this standard.

## 1. Purpose
This standard defines the password requirements for Brightwick user accounts.

## 2. Password requirements
- Passwords must be at least 12 characters long.
- Passwords must contain at least one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one number and one symbol.
- Passwords must be changed every 90 days. Users are reminded 14 days before their password expires.
- The last 5 passwords cannot be reused.

## 3. Account lockout
Accounts are locked after 10 failed sign-in attempts. Locked accounts unlock automatically after 15 minutes.

## 4. Password resets
Users who forget their password call the IT Service Desk. The analyst verifies the caller's identity by asking for their employee ID and date of birth, then sets a temporary password that must be changed at the next sign-in.

## 5. Multi-factor authentication
MFA is required for email and the VPN. SMS codes are an accepted MFA method.

## 6. Service accounts
Service account passwords must be at least 16 characters and changed every 180 days.
