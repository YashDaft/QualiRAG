# KB-1015: Remote Access for Contractors

Applies to: Contractors, agency staff and their sponsoring managers
Owner: Identity and Access Management | Last reviewed: 2026-06-02

## Summary
Contractors do not receive BrightSecure VPN access. They connect to Brightwick systems through Brightwick Remote Workspace, a browser-based virtual desktop available at workspace.brightwick.example. A company laptop is only issued when the contract explicitly requires one.

## Requesting an account
The sponsoring manager, who must be a permanent Brightwick employee, submits a "Contractor account" request in ServicePoint. The request must include the contract end date, the team the contractor joins and the systems they need. Accounts are created within 2 business days of approval.

## Account expiry
Contractor accounts expire automatically on the contract end date, and never last longer than 6 months. To extend access, the sponsor must submit a renewal request at least 5 business days before the expiry date. If the renewal is late, the account is disabled and must be re-enabled through a new request.

## Security requirements
- MFA is mandatory, using the company authenticator app. Hardware security keys are not issued to contractors.
- File downloads, file uploads and clipboard copy between Remote Workspace and the contractor's own device are disabled by default. An exception requires approval from the Security team.
- Contractors may not access Restricted data unless they are named in a signed data access agreement.

## Troubleshooting
If Remote Workspace shows a black screen, close all browser tabs, clear the browser cache and sign in again. If the session disconnects repeatedly, test on another network before raising a ticket. Contractors raise tickets through their sponsor or by calling the IT Service Desk.
