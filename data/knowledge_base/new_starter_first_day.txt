KB-1060: YOUR FIRST DAY: IT SETUP
=================================

Applies to: New employees

Owner: IT Service Desk | Last reviewed: 2026-08-12

Collect your equipment
----------------------
Headquarters staff collect their laptop from the IT Bar on Level 2, which is open 08:30 to 17:30 on weekdays. Remote staff receive their laptop by courier before their start date. Store staff do not receive a laptop; they use the store's StoreHub PC.

Activate your account
---------------------
On the morning of your first day, your manager receives a Temporary Access Pass for you. Use it to sign in to MyAccess, then set your password. Passwords must be at least 14 characters.

Register MFA within 24 hours
----------------------------
Install the company authenticator app and register it in MyAccess within 24 hours of your first sign-in. If you do not, your account is locked until the IT Service Desk verifies your identity.

Complete security training
--------------------------
Complete the Security Awareness course in the learning portal within 7 days of your start date.

Getting software
----------------
Install business software from the Software Portal. Anything not listed there must be requested through ServicePoint.

Getting help
------------
Contact the IT Service Desk through ServicePoint, by phone on extension 4357, or in person at the IT Bar.
