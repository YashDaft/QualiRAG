# KB-1040: Requesting Access to a Shared Mailbox

Applies to: All employees
Owner: Messaging Team | Last reviewed: 2026-02-17

## How to request access
Submit the "Shared mailbox access" form in ServicePoint. Name the mailbox, the type of access you need and the business reason.

## Approval
The mailbox owner must approve every request. If the owner has left the company, the request goes to the owner's former manager.

## Types of access
- Full Access: read and manage messages in the mailbox.
- Send As: send email that appears to come directly from the mailbox. Send As requires the owner's approval and a written business reason.
- Send on Behalf: send email that shows "on behalf of" the mailbox.

## How long it takes
Access is applied within 1 business day after approval. The mailbox then appears in the desktop mail client automatically within 60 minutes. If it does not appear, restart the mail client.

## New shared mailboxes
Creating a new shared mailbox requires approval from the department head, and every shared mailbox must have at least two owners. Shared mailboxes have no password and cannot be signed in to directly.
