# KB-1053: Receipt Printer Not Printing

Applies to: Store employees
Owner: Retail Systems | Last reviewed: 2026-09-01

## About receipt printers
Receipt printers at the tills are thermal printers connected to the POS terminal by USB. They are not network printers and do not use Follow-Me printing.

## Paper
Receipt printers use 80 mm thermal paper rolls. Order paper through store supplies, not through IT. Faint or blank receipts usually mean the wrong paper type, or a roll loaded the wrong way round.

## If nothing prints
1. Open the cover and check that the roll is loaded with the shiny side facing the print head.
2. Close the cover firmly until it clicks.
3. Turn the printer off and on.
4. Reprint the last receipt from the POS menu.

## If it still fails
Raise a P3 ticket in ServicePoint with the POS terminal ID and the printer serial number. In the meantime, offer customers an email receipt from the POS screen.
