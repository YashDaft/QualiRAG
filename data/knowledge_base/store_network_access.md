# KB-1012: Network Access for Store Staff

Applies to: Store employees and store managers
Owner: IT Service Desk | Last reviewed: 2026-07-14

## Summary
Store employees do not use the BrightSecure VPN. Store systems such as the point-of-sale (POS) terminals, the StoreHub back-office PC and the handheld scanners connect through the dedicated store network, which IT manages centrally from headquarters.

## How store staff reach company systems
- Use the StoreHub back-office PC for rostering, stock counts and the HR self-service portal.
- The shared store login may only be used on POS terminals. Every other system requires your personal account.
- Company email for store staff is available in the web client on StoreHub, or on a personal phone enrolled in Brightwick Mobile (see KB-1031).

## VPN access for store managers
Store managers who need HQ applications away from the store, for example regional sales reporting, may request BrightSecure VPN access through ServicePoint. The request must be approved by the regional operations manager. Store employees other than store managers are not eligible for VPN access.

## Wi-Fi in stores
The BW-Store-Ops network is reserved for store devices. Personal phones must use BW-Guest. Connecting a personal device to BW-Store-Ops is a policy breach, and the device is blocked automatically by the network access controls.

## When to raise a ticket
If StoreHub cannot reach company systems but the POS terminals are working, raise a P3 ticket in ServicePoint. If the whole store is offline, call the IT Service Desk and follow the Store Network Outage Runbook. Do not restart the store router unless the IT Service Desk asks you to.
