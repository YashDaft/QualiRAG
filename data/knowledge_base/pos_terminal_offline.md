# KB-1050: POS Terminal Shows "Offline"

Applies to: Store employees and store managers
Owner: Retail Systems | Last reviewed: 2026-09-01

## Offline mode
When a POS terminal loses its connection, an "Offline" banner appears at the top of the screen. The terminal keeps working in offline mode. It accepts cash and card payments and stores the card transactions, then sends them when the connection returns. Offline mode can hold up to 4 hours of card transactions. After 4 hours, card payments are blocked on that terminal and only cash can be accepted.

## If only one terminal is offline
1. Check the other terminals. If they are online, the problem is local to this terminal.
2. Check that the network cable is firmly connected at both ends.
3. Restart the terminal by holding the power button for 10 seconds, then sign in again.
4. If it is still offline, raise a P3 ticket in ServicePoint with the terminal ID printed on the sticker underneath the terminal.

## If every terminal in the store is offline
The whole store has lost its connection. Call the IT Service Desk, which logs a P2 incident and follows the Store Network Outage Runbook. Do not restart the store router unless the IT Service Desk asks you to.

## Card details
Never write down card numbers to process later. If card payments are blocked, accept cash or ask the customer to return.
