# KB-1031: Company Email on Personal Phones

Applies to: Employees and contractors using a personal phone
Owner: Endpoint Engineering | Last reviewed: 2026-08-04

## Brightwick Mobile is required
Personal phones can access company email and chat only after the phone is enrolled in Brightwick Mobile, the company app protection service. Install Brightwick Mobile from your phone's app store and sign in with your company account.

## Phone requirements
- An operating system that is the current or the previous major version.
- A screen lock PIN of at least 6 digits, or biometric unlock backed by a PIN.
- The phone must not be rooted or jailbroken.

## What IT can and cannot see
Company data is kept inside the managed apps. IT can remove company data from your phone with a selective wipe, but cannot see your personal photos, messages or apps. Brightwick never performs a full wipe of a personal device.

## Personal laptops and tablets
Personal laptops and tablets may not access company email or other company systems. They can only use BW-Guest Wi-Fi for internet access.

## When you leave Brightwick
Company data is removed from your phone automatically when your account is disabled.
