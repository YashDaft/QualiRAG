# KB-1020: Wi-Fi Networks at Brightwick Locations

Applies to: All employees, contractors and visitors
Owner: Network Engineering | Last reviewed: 2026-05-21

## Available networks
- BW-Corp: for company laptops only. It connects automatically using a device certificate, so there is no password to enter.
- BW-Guest: for visitors and personal devices. It provides internet access only, with no access to internal systems.
- BW-Store-Ops: for store devices only, such as POS terminals and handheld scanners. It is not available at headquarters.

## BW-Guest details
The BW-Guest password changes every Monday. At headquarters it is shown on the reception screens, and in stores it is posted on the back-office notice board. Guest sessions expire after 12 hours, after which the device must sign in again.

## If your laptop will not connect to BW-Corp
1. Check that the date and time on the laptop are correct. A wrong clock causes certificate errors.
2. Turn Wi-Fi off and on, then restart the laptop.
3. Make sure the laptop has connected to a wired network or the VPN in the last 60 days. Device certificates renew automatically only when the laptop can reach Brightwick systems.
4. If it still fails, raise a P3 ticket in ServicePoint and include the laptop asset tag and the error message.

## Working away from Brightwick locations
At home or in public places, connect to any trusted network and then sign in to the BrightSecure VPN before you access internal systems. Do not connect a company laptop to BW-Guest at headquarters, because internal systems are not reachable from it.
