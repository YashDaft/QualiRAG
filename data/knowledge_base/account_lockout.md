# KB-1063: Account Locked After Failed Sign-Ins

Applies to: All employees and contractors
Owner: Identity and Access Management | Last reviewed: 2026-07-30

## Why accounts lock
Your account locks after 5 failed sign-in attempts. This protects it from password-guessing attacks.

## Unlocking your account
- Wait 30 minutes. The account unlocks automatically.
- Or unlock it straight away in MyAccess by selecting "Unlock account" and completing MFA.

## Accounts that keep locking
Repeated lockouts are usually caused by an old password saved somewhere, for example in the mail app on your phone or in a saved mapped drive. After a password change, update every place where the old password is stored.

## If you were not signing in
If your account locks when you were not using it, someone may be trying to guess your password. Report it to the IT Service Desk so the Security Operations Center can investigate.

## Verification
The IT Service Desk never asks for your password. To confirm who you are, the analyst sends an MFA prompt to your registered device or verifies you through your manager.
