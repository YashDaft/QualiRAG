KB-1052: HANDHELD BARCODE SCANNER PROBLEMS
==========================================

Applies to: Store employees

Owner: Retail Systems | Last reviewed: 2026-09-01

Pairing a scanner
-----------------
Handheld scanners pair with their charging dock and with the StoreHub app.

  1) Place the scanner in its dock for 10 seconds.
  2) Scan the "Pair" barcode printed on the label on the dock.
  3) Two short beeps mean the scanner is paired. One long beep means pairing failed, so repeat the steps.

Battery
-------
If the scanner turns off during a shift, swap the battery with a charged spare. Spare batteries are kept charging in the back office.

Scanner does not read barcodes
------------------------------
  * Clean the scanner window with a dry cloth.
  * Check that the scanner is not in Inventory mode. The mode is shown at the top of the screen.
  * Try scanning a different product to rule out a damaged label.

Replacing a faulty scanner
--------------------------
Take a scanner from the store's spare pool and pair it. Raise a P4 ticket if a spare was available, or a P3 ticket if the store has no working spare. Return the faulty scanner in the replacement box when it arrives.
