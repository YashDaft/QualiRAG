REMOTE WORK GUIDELINES 2024
===========================

Document ID: HR-IT-RW-2024 | Version: 1.0 | Effective date: 2024-02-01

Owner: HR and IT | Classification: Internal

Status: SUPERSEDED on 2026-01-15 by the Acceptable Use Policy v2.0 (POL-SEC-003). Retained for reference only.

1. Purpose
----------
These guidelines help employees work safely from home and while travelling.

2. Devices
----------
Employees should use their company laptop for remote work wherever possible. Employees without a company laptop may use a personal laptop to open company webmail and the HR portal in a web browser. Personal laptops must have antivirus software installed and up to date.

3. Connecting to company systems
--------------------------------
Connect to the VPN from your company laptop before using internal systems. The VPN is not needed for webmail or the HR portal.

4. Home and public networks
---------------------------
Home Wi-Fi must use WPA2 or stronger encryption and must not use the router's default password. Public Wi-Fi may be used for webmail without the VPN. Use the VPN on public Wi-Fi for everything else.

5. Printing at home
-------------------
Internal documents may be printed at home. Confidential documents must not be printed at home.

6. Working abroad
-----------------
Tell your manager before working from another country for more than 2 weeks.

7. Getting help
---------------
Contact the IT Service Desk by phone or email for remote work problems.
