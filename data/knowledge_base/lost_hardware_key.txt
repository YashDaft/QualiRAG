KB-1033: LOST OR DAMAGED HARDWARE SECURITY KEY
==============================================

Applies to: Employees who have been issued a hardware security key

Owner: Identity and Access Management | Last reviewed: 2026-03-11

Who has hardware keys
---------------------
Hardware security keys are issued to cloud administrators, privileged account holders and Finance staff who approve payments. Contractors are not issued hardware keys.

Report it straight away
-----------------------
Report a lost, stolen or damaged key immediately, and no later than 1 hour after you notice, by calling the IT Service Desk. The key is revoked as soon as it is reported, so it can no longer be used to sign in.

Signing in while you wait
-------------------------
If the company authenticator app is registered on your account, use it for normal sign-ins. If the key was your only method, the IT Service Desk can issue a Temporary Access Pass after verifying your identity, as described in KB-1030.

Privileged administrator accounts require a hardware key. Until the replacement arrives, holders of privileged accounts must not use them, unless the Security team grants a time-limited exception.

Replacement
-----------
Collect a replacement key from the IT Bar on Level 2 at headquarters, or ask for it to be couriered. Replacements are normally available within 3 business days.

If you find the old key
-----------------------
Do not use it again. A revoked key cannot be re-registered. Return it to the IT Bar so it can be destroyed securely.
