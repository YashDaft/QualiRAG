# KB-1030: Moving MFA to a New Phone

Applies to: Employees and contractors who use the company authenticator app
Owner: Identity and Access Management | Last reviewed: 2026-07-30

## Before you change phones
Set up the new phone while your old phone still works:
1. Install the company authenticator app on the new phone.
2. Sign in to MyAccess and open Security info.
3. Select Add sign-in method, choose the authenticator app and scan the QR code with the new phone.
4. Approve a test prompt on the new phone, then remove the old phone from Security info.
5. Only then reset or hand in the old phone.

## If your old phone is lost, broken or already wiped
Call the IT Service Desk. Because your MFA is not available, the analyst must verify your identity another way. This is done through a video call with your manager present, or through a callback to your manager's registered phone number. After verification, the analyst issues a Temporary Access Pass (TAP). A TAP is valid for 8 hours and lets you sign in to MyAccess to register the new phone.

## SMS
SMS codes are not an approved everyday method. SMS can only be used when IT has explicitly enabled it as a recovery method on your account.

## Never share codes
The IT Service Desk will never ask you to read out an MFA code or a TAP that was sent to you. If someone asks, end the call and report it using KB-1027.
