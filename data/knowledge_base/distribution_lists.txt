KB-1041: DISTRIBUTION LISTS
===========================

Applies to: All employees

Owner: Messaging Team | Last reviewed: 2026-02-17

Creating a list
---------------
Request a new distribution list through ServicePoint. The list owner must be a permanent employee. Two owners are recommended so that the list is not orphaned when someone leaves.

Members
-------
Lists can include employees and contractors. Adding external email addresses requires a Security review, because external members receive internal information.

Managing membership
-------------------
Owners can add and remove members themselves in MyAccess under Groups. A ticket is not needed for membership changes.

Restrictions
------------
  * Lists with more than 500 members only accept messages from approved senders.
  * Company-wide lists, such as All Staff, can only be used by Internal Communications.
