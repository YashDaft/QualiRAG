KB-1070: SCREEN LOCK AND CLEAN DESK
===================================

Applies to: All employees and contractors

Owner: Information Security | Last reviewed: 2026-01-20

Screen lock
-----------
Company laptops lock automatically after 10 minutes without activity. Lock your screen yourself every time you leave your desk by pressing Windows key + L. Store staff must sign out of POS terminals at the end of each shift.

Clean desk
----------
  * Keep Confidential and Restricted printouts in a locked drawer when you are away from your desk.
  * Dispose of printed documents in the secure shredding bins, not in normal recycling.
  * Do not leave notes with passwords or account details on desks or monitors.

Visitors
--------
Visitors must sign in at reception and be escorted by a Brightwick employee at all times.

Badges
------
Never lend your building badge to anyone. A lost badge must be reported to Facilities the same day.
